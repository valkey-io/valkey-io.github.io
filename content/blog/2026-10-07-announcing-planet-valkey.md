TL;DR: [Planet Valkey](https://planet.valkey.io/) replaces [Planet for the Valkey Community](https://valkey-demo.oursqlcommunity.org/).
If you were following the old site, update your bookmarks because Planet for the Valkey Community will eventually disappear.

TL;DR2: If you enjoy Planet Valkey, please consider starring the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/valkey-io/planet/) and following us on social media ([Mastodon](https://fosstodon.org/@Planet_Valkey), [BlueSky Bridge](https://bsky.app/profile/Planet-Valkey.fosstodon.org.ap.brid.gy), and [X/Twitter](https://x.com/Planet_Valkey)).

I am pleased to announce the availability of [Planet Valkey: a single destination for Valkey content](https://planet.valkey.io/).

Planet Valkey brings together content from across the ecosystem, including official project news, community blogs, cloud providers, vendors, media outlets, and independent contributors.
Instead of following dozens of websites or hoping social media surfaces the content you care about, you can keep track of the latest Valkey developments from a single location.

The website is available at:
- Planet Valkey: [https://planet.valkey.io/](https://planet.valkey.io/)
- Atom Feed: [https://planet.valkey.io/atom.xml](https://planet.valkey.io/atom.xml)

The best way to follow Planet Valkey is through its Atom Feed using a feed reader such as [CommaFeed](https://www.commafeed.com/), [Feeder](https://feeder.co/) or [Feedly](https://feedly.com/news-reader).
There are many other excellent feed readers available as well ([link1](https://www.reddit.com/r/rss/comments/1nfvw5o/what_are_the_best_rss_feed_reader_apps_with_a/), [link2](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41378686), [link3](https://www.readless.app/blog/best-rss-readers-2026)).
You could also follow the [#planet-feed](https://app.slack.com/client/THDV9665P/C0C19F053CP) channel in the [Valkey Community Slack](https://valkey.io/slack), but I do not recommend it because [Slack is a Suboptimal Feed Reader](https://jfg-mysql.blogspot.com/2025/11/slack-suboptimal-feed-reader.html) (you will miss news).
If you prefer a simpler approach, you can always visit the Planet Valkey [website](https://planet.valkey.io/) directly and browse the latest posts.

Planet for the Valkey Community was launched as an experimental demonstration.
While it fulfilled its purpose, the URL (valkey-demo.oursqlcommunity.org) was never intended to be permanent (reusing the [Planet for the MySQL Community](https://planet.oursqlcommunity.org/) domain was convenient for the demo while deciding on a better home for the project).
The new site is hosted under a URL more closely associated with the Valkey Project, gets rid of the "demo" branding, and has a cleaner name.

Planet Valkey is an open community project to which everyone can contribute.
If you publish Valkey-related content and would like it to appear on Planet Valkey, proposing a new feed is easy: open an [issue](https://github.com/valkey-io/planet/issues) or submit a [Pull Request](https://github.com/valkey-io/planet/pulls).
If you would like to learn more, propose a feed, report issues, or contribute improvements, visit the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/valkey-io/planet/).
If you want to discuss the project, join the [#planet-discussion](https://app.slack.com/client/THDV9665P/C0C4NS3SDQV) channel in the [Valkey Community Slack](https://valkey.io/slack).
If you enjoy the project, please consider starring the [repository](https://github.com/valkey-io/planet/) and following us on social media ([Mastodon](https://fosstodon.org/@Planet_Valkey), [BlueSky Bridge](https://bsky.app/profile/Planet-Valkey.fosstodon.org.ap.brid.gy), and [X/Twitter](https://x.com/Planet_Valkey)).

Finally, I would like to [thank AWS Open Source](https://jfg-mysql.blogspot.com/2026/04/thanks-aws-open-source.html) for providing two years’ worth of cloud-hosting credits.
Their support helps make community infrastructure projects like Planet Valkey possible.
