<h2>Last Year, We Took Over Taco Bell</h2>

<p>Last year, House of Valkey took over three floors of Taco Bell Cantina in Las Vegas with a custom menu, and spiked Baja Blasts. Turns out, the people you know from GitHub are very good company outside a comment thread. Following a three-story Taco Bell takeover takes some thought, so this year we found a living room big enough for the family.</p>

<h2>Make Yourself at Home</h2>

<p>HaSalon means “the living room,” which feels appropriate for a community whose usual gathering places involve browser tabs. We love a good GitHub thread, but sometimes you want to finish a conversation without opening another issue.</p>

<p>On December 1, we’re getting together to catch up, put faces to familiar handles, and finally talk without calculating somebody else’s time zone. We’ve also been working on a few reasons to leave some room in your suitcase.</p>

<h2>You’re Part of the Family</h2>

<p>Valkey brings together people who write code, run it, build businesses around it, explain it to their coworkers, and ask questions that make the whole project better. House of Valkey is for all of them, including the people who are still learning what Valkey is.</p>

<p>Bring your team or the friend who’s heard you say “Valkey” enough times to finally investigate. Come solo and give us a chance to introduce you around. Nobody needs a merged pull request to get a welcome here, but everyone needs to register.</p>

<p>In Vegas for AWS re:Invent, living nearby, or just happening to be in town? Come spend the evening with us. Your conference badge can take the night off.</p>
