Nitai Caro is a software engineer at AWS ElastiCache, based out of Seattle, WA.
He contributed forkless save and forkless full sync to Valkey, as well as other replication features, such as the threaded full sync RDB download on replicas.
When not working on Valkey, Nitai enjoys cooking and traveling.
