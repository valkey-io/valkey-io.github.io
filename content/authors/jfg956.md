Jean-François Gagné (J-F, JFG, or Jeff) is a MySQL Community Contributor who is also active in the Valkey Community.
After years of maintaining a [News Aggregator for the MySQL Community](https://planet.oursqlcommunity.org/), he applied that experience to create what would become [Planet Valkey](https://planet.valkey.io/).
J-F continues to maintain Planet Valkey as a single destination for news, articles, and other content from the Valkey community.
More about his MySQL background and contributions can be found on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jfg956/).
