Sasidharan Gopal is a knowledge-seeker who geeks out on infrastructure, with over 12 years of experience in Linux systems and a current focus at the intersection of databases, Kubernetes, SRE, and cloud infrastructure. He works on running Redis and Valkey at scale — provisioning, security patching, and performance tuning across Sentinel and Cluster deployments — and has co-built and maintained Kubernetes operators for both. He contributes upstream to [Valkey GLIDE](https://github.com/valkey-io/valkey-glide), Redis Exporter, and [Envoy](https://github.com/freshworks-oss/envoy/tree/v1.28.0-dbaas-RELEASE), and has led Kubernetes (EKS) upgrades, including a [blue-green migration approach](https://medium.com/freshworks-engineering-blog/fast-k8s-upgrades-9cb60be7f93e) for zero-downtime cluster transitions.

Outside of work, he's a mad cricket fan who also plays table tennis and snooker, and is usually a few episodes into whatever Netflix series is trending.
