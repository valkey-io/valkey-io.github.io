Björn Svensson is an Open Source Developer at Ericsson with over 20 years of experience in the telecom industry, dedicating the last 6 years exclusively to open source development across a wide range of projects. He is a maintainer of Valkey clients and active in the valkey-operator project since start. Björn is passionate about building reliable, community-driven open source infrastructure and bridging the gap between open source innovation and enterprise needs.
