Aditi Gupta is a Software Engineer II at JioHotstar, where she builds and operates distributed backend systems powering one of the world's largest streaming platforms (72M+ peak concurrent viewers). Her work focuses on distributed systems, production reliability, observability, and high-throughput services. She frequently presents at Linux Foundation conferences, including the flagship KubeCon and Open Source Summit events on operating resilient cloud-native platforms and large-scale distributed systems.
