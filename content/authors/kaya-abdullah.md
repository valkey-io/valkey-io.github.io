Abdullah maintains LibreDB Studio, an open source database GUI that runs in the browser and connects to Valkey alongside the relational and document databases a team already runs. Most of that work is about presenting engines that behave nothing alike through one interface without pretending they are the same, which is where the Valkey key browser in this post came from.
