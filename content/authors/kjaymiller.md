Jay Miller is a Developer Advocate and a founder of Black Python Devs.

Jay is a recognized leader in the Python community globally and has advocated for other communities such as PostgreSQL and Valkey.

When not writing or writing about code, Jay can often be seen cheering on their local baseball team, or spending time with their family, pets, and many many houseplants.
