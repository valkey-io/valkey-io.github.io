**Daniel Moran** is a software engineer with more than 20 years of experience and the maintainer of [fakeredis](https://github.com/cunla/fakeredis-py), the in-memory test double that includes `FakeValkey`.
He is a founding member of [Django Commons](https://github.com/django-commons) and has built backend and cloud systems at Amazon, Audible, and Dell EMC.

He runs DSoftware Inc., a consultancy focused on Python, Java Virtual Machine (JVM), and Amazon Web Services (AWS) systems.
