Alon Arenberg is a Software Engineer at AWS working on Valkey, where he spends most of his time on observability: making the server able to explain what it is doing and why a workload behaves the way it does.

He contributed server-side hot key detection to Valkey and is interested in the gap between the metrics a database emits and the questions an operator actually has at three in the morning.
